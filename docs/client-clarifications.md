# What to confirm with the client

The registration module can be demonstrated now. These are the decisions still owed by the Organising Committee. Send this list as written.

## Accounts

There is no public sign-up. Someone at the Committee has to be the Super admin, and that person creates every other account.

| Role | How many | What they can do | What to ask for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Super admin | Two named people | Creates accounts, and can also approve or reject | Name and email for each. A second person matters: only a Super admin can reset someone else's password. |
| Registration reviewer | As many as they want | Approves, rejects, or sends a record back. Cannot create accounts. | Names and emails. |
| Delegation officer | One per country | Registers that country only. Signs in on the public site, not in the Control Room. | For each of the nine countries: the officer's name, email, phone, and the institution they represent. |
| Athlete, coach, official | None | No login. The officer enters them. | Confirm that one officer per country is enough. A second officer for the same country is not possible today. |

Also ask how the temporary password should reach the officer. The system can set one, and a person shares it privately. It does not email an invitation by itself.

## Documents

Passport, photograph, and the guardian file for minors are always required. Ask which of these are also required, and whether that is for every sport or only some:

- Sports eligibility confirmation
- Medical fitness certificate
- Anything else

## Events

Discipline is a free-text box. Ask them to send the event list per sport when they have it, or to confirm that free text is acceptable until then.

## Where the records live

Real passports and photos of minors cannot be stored on the live site until they approve a database. Ask them to confirm that the Committee will host that database, and which address should receive the notice when a record is submitted, approved, or sent back. Until a mail service is connected, the officer only sees that notice on their own dashboard.

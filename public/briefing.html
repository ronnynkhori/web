Delegation
registration module

One secure account per country. The officer registers athletes and officials. The Organising Committee reviews everyone in one place. Portuguese and English.

Who does what

Four people. Two accounts.

WhoWhereWhat they do
Super adminControl Room → UsersCreates one officer account per country
Delegation officer/pt/delegation or /en/delegationRegisters that country’s athletes and officials
Super admin or Registration reviewerControl Room → RegistrationsApproves, rejects, or sends a record back
AthleteNowhereHas no account

Status

One path for every person

Draft→ Submitted→ Under review→ Correction required→ Approved or Rejected

The officer can edit a draft and a record that was sent back. A submitted record stays locked until the Committee asks for a correction.

The officer

What gets registered

The delegationCountry, institution, head of delegation, officer, sports entered, and estimated numbers.
Each athletePassport identity, sport, discipline, categories, federation, club, jersey, emergency contact, accessibility, and any other sport note.
Each officialHead, coach, assistant, manager, medical, physio, admin, judge, or other. Photograph and contact.
DocumentsPassport, photograph, guardian authorisation for minors, plus eligibility, medical, or other files when the Committee marks them required. PDF, JPG, or PNG. Not public links.

The Committee

What the Control Room does

  1. 1
    Dashboard Athletes, officials, and how many are waiting, sent back, approved, rejected, or still drafts.
  2. 2
    Registrations Open a person, then approve, reject, or send the record back with a note.
  3. 3
    Quotas and documents Set how many men, women, officials and judges each sport accepts, and which extra documents are required.
  4. 4
    Users and activity One officer per country. Every save and every decision is in the activity log.

Before a record can be submitted

The checks

Required fieldsA blank mandatory field stops the submit.
AgeUnder 18 on 25 March 2027 needs a parent or guardian and their authorisation. Age bands follow the programme.
No duplicateThe same passport or ID cannot be active twice in one country.
QuotaA country cannot go past the number set for that sport. The starting numbers are the provisional programme.
ExcelDownload the template, fill athletes, import them as drafts, then add documents on each person.
ExportExcel download, and a print page the browser saves as PDF.

After approval

One number. One record.

Approval issues CPLP27-AGO-0001, built as CPLP27, the country code, and a sequence. The accreditation page shows that number, the photograph, and a QR code of the number. Nobody registers again to produce it.
